# Warranty

Every Northwind Robotics arm and gripper comes with a two-year limited warranty that covers manufacturing defects in motors, joints and controller boards. The warranty does not cover damage caused by collisions, liquid spills, or firmware that was modified by the customer.

Customers on the Enterprise plan get an extended warranty of five years and a next-business-day replacement for failed units. To open a warranty claim, send the serial number and a short video of the fault to the support portal; a technician answers within one business day.
