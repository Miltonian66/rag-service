# Plans and pricing

Northwind offers three software plans for fleet management. Starter costs 29 euros per robot per month and includes the dashboard and basic alerts. Pro costs 79 euros per robot per month and adds the Fleet API, scheduled tasks and role-based access. Enterprise pricing is negotiated per contract and includes single sign-on, the extended warranty and a dedicated support engineer.

Annual billing gives a 20 percent discount on Starter and Pro. Educational institutions receive a 50 percent discount on any plan after verification.
