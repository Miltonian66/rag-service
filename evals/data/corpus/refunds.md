# Refund policy

Customers can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase for any Northwind Robotics hardware product, as long as the device is returned in its original packaging. After 30 days and up to 90 days, refunds are issued as store credit only, minus a 15 percent restocking fee.

Software subscriptions are refunded pro rata for the unused months when a customer cancels an annual plan. Monthly plans are not refunded; they simply stop renewing at the end of the current billing period.

Refunds are paid back to the original payment method within 5 business days after the returned device passes inspection at the warehouse in Rotterdam.
