# Shipping and delivery

Orders placed before 14:00 Central European Time ship the same business day from the Rotterdam warehouse. Standard delivery inside the European Union takes 2 to 4 business days and is free for orders above 500 euros.

Express delivery is available for a flat fee of 49 euros and arrives the next business day in most EU countries. Shipments to the United Kingdom, Norway and Switzerland need a customs declaration, which Northwind prepares automatically; import duties are paid by the recipient.
